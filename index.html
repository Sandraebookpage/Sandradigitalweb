About the author

Sandra C. Ukaegbu

I am a climate change professional and UNFCCC Technical Expert Reviewer under the Paris Agreement’s Enhanced Transparency Framework (ETF), with practical experience in the technical review of Biennial Transparency Reports (BTRs) and national greenhouse gas inventory reporting. I have participated in the technical review of BTRs for several countries and have experience in reviewing the Agriculture, LULUCF and Waste sectors, as well as undertaking examinations related to Nationally Determined Contributions (NDCs).

My professional interests include climate change mitigation and adaptation, climate policy, transparency, capacity building and international cooperation. I am particularly interested in contributing to strengthened technical capacity, evidence-based climate action and effective implementation of climate commitments, especially in the African context.
